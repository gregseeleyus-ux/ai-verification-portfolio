# Knowledge Card Template

Atomic unit of project intelligence. One finding, rule, or entity per card.

```yaml
---
title: "Descriptive title of the finding or entity"
id: "YYYYMMDDHHmm timestamp"
created: YYYY-MM-DD
updated: YYYY-MM-DD
project: "Project name"
tags:
  - "tag1"
status: verified | inferred | extrapolated | needs-verification
confidence: 0.0-1.0
sources:
  - "Source description with date"
---
```

## Summary
Two or three sentences. The bottom line and why it matters.

## Key Findings
Numbered. Each finding tagged verified, inferred, or extrapolated.

## Implications
What this means. Who needs to act. What changes.
