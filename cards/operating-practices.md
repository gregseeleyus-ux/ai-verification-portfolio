# Operating Practices: Daily Loop, Follow-up SLAs, and Throttles

A project knowledge card for turning pasted meeting notes, emails, and task dumps into scheduled work without drift. Load it in the project where you paste your raw inputs.

## 1) Daily loop (no drift)

- 2-minute triage (after each paste): tag 1 to 3 items as Do Next (48h); everything else goes to Backlog.
- One Comms Sprint plus one Deep Block: every workday, schedule (a) a 30 to 45 minute Comms Sprint and (b) a 60 to 90 minute Deep Block from your Do Next list.
- End-of-day close (3 minutes or less): "What must move tomorrow?" Schedule 1 block; archive today's wins.

## 2) Rally Mode (when you are behind)

- 15-minute rescue: paste the notes from your 2 to 3 most recent meetings and say "Rally Mode."
- Output: top 5 actions total, 1 Comms Sprint, 1 Deep Block, and one-liner emails for the two most critical nudges.

## 3) Weekly rhythm (keep momentum)

- Friday Reset (20 to 30 minutes): clear Waiting On, prune duplicates, promote 3 priorities to Next Week's Big 3.
- Monday Launch (15 minutes): schedule the week's two Deep Blocks first, then place Comms Sprints around them.

## 4) Follow-up SLA (never let threads die)

- External deliverables: respond or ship within 2 business days.
- Waiting On nudges: ping at 48 to 72 hours; auto-escalate at 7 days (escalation nudge below).
- Unknown due date: set a review_on date 3 to 5 days out.

## 5) Calendar block presets (paste these often)

- Comms Sprint (30 to 45 minutes): write and send 3 to 5 nudges; capture replies.
- Review Sprint (60 minutes): read notes and produce 1 to 2 artifacts.
- Build Block (90 minutes): outline and draft a doc or deliverable.

Use the same titles repeatedly; it lowers friction.

## 6) Tiny tag dictionary (stay consistent)

- Client: Client-A, Partner-B
- Project: Project-A, Project-B, Project-C
- Topic: Funding, Contracting, Data, Scheduling

Replace the placeholders with your own short, fixed tags and do not improvise new ones.

## 7) File naming (so you can relink fast)

YYYY-MM-DD_Client_Project_Topic.txt

Paste this on top of each run:

Source: YYYY-MM-DD_Client-A_Project-A_Notes.txt

## 8) Prioritization throttle (avoid overload)

- Cap Do Next (48h) at 7 items or fewer.
- Use NOW / NEXT / LATER tags in the readable list; only NOW gets blocks today.

## 9) Decision micro-journal (zero overhead)

For any decision detected, append one line:

Decision: [who] [what] [when]; why: <rationale> (source ref)

## 10) Failure modes and guardrails

- Too many tasks? Merge duplicates; batch small comms.
- No owners or dates? Assign the user and set review_on.
- No source references? Mark "no reference available" once; do not stall extraction.

## 11) Light metrics (proof you are winning)

- Momentum Ratio (daily): completed Do Next items divided by scheduled blocks. Aim for 0.7 or higher.
- Aging watch: any user-owned item older than 7 days auto-escalates (see template B).

## Copy-ready snippets

A) Micro-plan (drop at the end of each run)

- Today: [Block A], [Comms Sprint]
- Tomorrow: [Block B], [1 key nudge]
- This week: [Big 3 outcomes]

B) Escalation nudge (paste into email or DM)

Quick bump on [item] discussed [date]. We are aiming for [target]. Can you confirm [owner] to [next step] by [date]? (Ref: [source note])

C) Calendar description template (reuse)

Goal: <one sentence outcome>
Inputs: <meeting notes or summary; file name if link expires>
Deliverables: <bullet list>
Notes: <source refs and constraints>
