# Benchmark-Anchored Forecast Sheet

Status: DESIGNED. Generalized from one forecast instrument; this general form has not been used yet.

## What it is

A one- or two-page forecast sheet that a team fills in before a known outcome event. Everyone locks in guesses against the same questions, and the sheet shows calibration panels built from a historical benchmark. The method is in how the questions get chosen: anchor to a benchmark, cut redundant and low-variance questions, stand in proxies for signals you cannot measure directly, and review the list twice before building. The sheet is disposable. The method is the reusable part.

## When to use it

- A team is waiting on a dated outcome with several measurable parts, for example a product launch, a quarterly result, an award decision, or a fundraising close.
- You want people to commit to explicit, scoreable expectations beforehand, both for engagement and to show later where the group's read was off.

## How to run it

1. **Anchor to a historical benchmark.** Pick the most recent comparable event. Put it on the sheet as a calibration panel, together with current baseline figures and the latest interim data. Label the panels as sanity checks: they do not tell anyone the answer.
2. **Draft candidate questions, then screen them.**
   - Cut for redundancy: if two questions would be settled by the same number, keep one.
   - Cut for low variance: drop questions that nearly everyone will answer the same way, because they do not separate forecasters.
3. **Proxy what you cannot measure.** If the thing you care about will not be reported directly (for example, a wave of new participants), ask about an observable stand-in (for example, the first-time participants' share of the total, or an over/under on a stated share).
4. **Group the questions and mix the formats.** Use sections such as headline result, breakdown by unit, signal indicators, secondary outcomes, contrarian checks on the main alternative, and a bonus. Mix numeric point estimates, over/under against a stated threshold, yes/no, pick-one-from-a-list, and time-of-event questions.
5. **Run two review passes on the question list before you build the sheet.** Do not build from the first draft.
6. **Put the rules on the sheet.** The closest answer in each category wins bragging rights. There is a tiebreaker (an exact raw number; decide in advance whether going over disqualifies). All guesses lock at a stated time. Participation is voluntary and no money changes hands; point to your organization's policy.
7. **Score after the event.** Collect the sheets at lock time, score each category against the actual results, then apply the tiebreaker.

## Output

A filled-in forecast sheet from each participant, and a scored results list after the event.

```
[Event name] Forecast Sheet   |   [event date]

Name: ____________________________   Submitted: ______________

Closest answer in each category wins bragging rights. Tiebreaker at the bottom.
All guesses final at [lock time]. Voluntary; no money. See [organization policy].

Calibration panels (sanity checks, not answers)
- Baseline now: [current base figures]
- Benchmark: [most recent comparable event and its results]
- Latest interim data: [figures, as of date]

Headline
1. [Headline result, numeric]                          ________
2. [Total volume, numeric]                             ________
3. [Time the result is final or announced]            ________

Breakdown by unit
4. [Which option wins unit X?]  [A] / [B]  (circle one)
5. [Margin in unit X]                                  ________
6. [Number of units won, out of N]                     ________

Signal indicators (proxies)
7. [Proxy share]: over / under [threshold]  (circle one)
8. [Proxy share, numeric]                              ________

Secondary outcomes
9.  [Secondary result: winner]                         ________
10. [Secondary result: margin]                         ________

Contrarian checks
11. [Main alternative's ceiling]: over / under [threshold]  (circle one)
12. [Does the main alternative lead in any unit outside its core segment?]  Yes / No

Bonus
13. [First source to report the result]  [list]  (circle one)

Tiebreaker
TB. [Exact raw number]                                 ________
```
