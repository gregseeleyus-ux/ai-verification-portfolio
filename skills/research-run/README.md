# research-run

Status: PROTOTYPE

Builds and judges deep research runs. Scopes each run to one gating question, requires discovery and disconfirming evidence rather than confirmation, demands two independent evidence chains with quoted passages, and ends with a ship, correct, rerun, or cut verdict. Includes nine use-case templates and a failure-mode library.

**Use it:** Any time you are about to launch deep research, or a research report comes back and you need to decide whether to trust it.

**Depends on:** guardian-context

**Note:** The files in references/ are older than SKILL.md: where they differ (skeleton elements, failure-mode count), SKILL.md governs.

**Install:** see docs/INSTALL.md. The skill itself is SKILL.md in this folder.
